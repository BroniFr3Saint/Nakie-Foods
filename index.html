Our Story

Bringing Authentic Ghanaian Flavours to Anaji, Takoradi

At Nakie Foods, we believe that food is more than just sustenance — it's a celebration of culture, community, and love. Nestled in the heart of Anaji, Takoradi, we serve time-honoured Ghanaian recipes passed down through generations.

From our rich, aromatic Jollof rice to our perfectly pounded Fufu, every dish is crafted with fresh, locally-sourced ingredients and a deep respect for tradition.
